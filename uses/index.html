UseLaserWhat it does
Metal 3D printing (laser powder bed fusion)
Aerospace / manufacturing · since 2004 (GE LEAP nozzle development)		Melts metal powder layer by layer; GE LEAP fuel nozzle consolidated about 20 parts into one, 25% lighter source
Laser guide stars
astronomy		TOPTICA-MPBC · sodium line · 22 W per unit (VLT, 2018)Excites sodium about 90 km up to make an artificial star for adaptive optics. source
Battery busbar and cell welding
Automotive / e-mobility		Welds busbars joining cells into modules and packs (Al/Al, Cu/Cu, Al/Cu), seals cells gas-tight, welds trays and housings source
Copper contact welding (green laser)
Automotive / e-mobility		Green-wavelength disk laserMaker says it welds copper contacts with practically no spatter source
Hairpin stator and drivetrain welding
Automotive / e-mobility		Welds hairpin stators for e-motors, rotor shafts, gears and fuel-cell bipolar plates source
Optical tweezers
biology and physics · since 1987		Focused laser light holds and moves particles, atoms, viruses and living cells. source
Telephone call over a laser beam
communications · since 1960-12-13		helium-neon · 1.15 micrometersJavan's team carried a telephone call over the first helium-neon beam the day after it first ran. source
Laser pointers
consumer		 · FDA: do not buy over 5 mW (2023 guidance)Hand-held pointers; many tested above their labeled power. source
Laser toys and cat toys
consumer		 · FDA: toys within IEC Class 1 (2023 guidance)FDA lists laser cat toys as an example of laser toys. source
Desktop engraving (open frame)
Consumer / hobby		Creality Falcon2 · 22 W, Class 4UK regulator found it excessively strong for consumer use and rejected the import at the border (2024) source
Desktop engraving and cutting (enclosed)
Consumer / hobby · since 2021 (xTool D1 launch)		Diode laser module (Bambu Lab H2D) · 455 nm and 850 nm · 10 W ± 1 W (maker spec)Class 4 module inside a machine rated Class 1 when its protection is complete and working source
BDXL discs
consumer electronics		 · 405 nmMultilayer Blu-ray: 100 GB (3 layers), 128 GB (4 layers). source
Blu-ray player
consumer electronics		 · 405 nmBlue-violet laser with a 0.85 numerical aperture lens; 25 GB single layer, 50 GB dual layer. source
CD player
consumer electronics		 · 780 nmReads pits on the disc; about 700 MB. source
DVD player
consumer electronics		 · 650 nmReads smaller pits; 4.7 GB single layer, 8.5 GB dual layer. source
Laser mouse
consumer electronics · since 2004		laser sensor (Agilent)A laser lights the desk for a tiny camera that tracks surface texture; 800 counts per inch. source
Phone and tablet lidar
consumer electronics · since 2020		VCSELTime-of-flight depth sensing to about 5 m for augmented reality, autofocus and Night mode portraits. source
Airborne missile-defense laser (Airborne Laser, YAL-1A)
Defense · since 2002		Chemical oxygen iodine laser (COIL) plus two solid-state lasers · Megawatt-class (official)A modified 747 tested against boosting missiles at Edwards AFB, 2002-2012; retired to storage in 2012. source
Counter-drone laser in combat (Israel)
Defense · since 2025		High-power laser (system disputed: Lite Beam, Iron Beam-M)Israel said about 40 drones were downed in 2025; the Ministry of Defense said no Iron Beam components were involved. source
Laser air defense against rockets, mortars and drones (Iron Beam)
Defense · since 2025		High-power laser (Rafael; laser by Elbit Systems)Declared operational September 2025; first combat-ready system delivered to the IDF in December 2025. source
Laser target designation for guided bombs
Defense · since 1968		A laser marks a target and a laser-guided bomb steers toward the spot; the BOLT-117 was combat-evaluated in 1968, followed by the Paveway kits. source
Long-range naval ocular interrupter
Defense · since 2024		Visible laserUS Navy Long Range Ocular Interrupter reached initial operational capability in fiscal 2024. source
Naval counter-drone laser (DragonFire)
Defense · since 2025		UK MOD says it downed drones at up to 650 km/h; due on a Type 45 destroyer by 2027; about GBP 10 per shot (official claim, 2025). source
Naval laser demonstrator (Germany)
Defense · since 2022		Installed on the frigate Sachsen in June 2022; more than 100 test firings, drones downed (company statement, 2023). source
Non-lethal dazzler (optical distracter)
Defense		Visible laser · Designed below maximum permissible exposure (official)Temporary, reversible glare to hail and warn at checkpoints and at sea; fielded US examples include the LA-9/P and GLARE MOUT. source
Shipboard laser weapon (LaWS)
Defense · since 2014		US Navy prototype on USS Ponce, Persian Gulf, Sept-Nov 2014; small boat and ScanEagle drone targets; under $1 per shot (official claim, 2014). source
Shipboard laser weapon with dazzler and surveillance (HELIOS)
Defense · since 2022		 · 60-plus kW class (reported, 2025)Lockheed Martin system on USS Preble; fired at a drone in FY2024 testing. source
Vehicle-mounted counter-drone laser (India Mk-II(A))
Defense · since 2025		 · 30 kW class (official)DRDO demonstration against a fixed-wing drone and swarm drones, 13 April 2025. source
Caries removal and restorations
dentistry		FDA has cleared lasers for removing decay and tooth-colored restorations. source
Root canal adjunct
dentistry		Cleared as an adjunct; clinical benefit of laser disinfection not known (AAE, 2019). source
Soft tissue dental surgery
dentistry		FDA has cleared lasers for gum and other soft tissue work. source
Fractional resurfacing

Hair removal
dermatology · since 1990s		RubyDeveloped by Anderson and Grossman; an expert calls it the most common laser treatment in medicine. source
Home hair-growth devices (LLLT)
dermatology · since 2007		Low-level red light · about 650 nm (620-678 nm)First FDA-cleared device for male pattern hair loss in 2007; 32 cleared home-use devices on the US market as of January 5, 2020. source
Port-wine stain treatment
dermatology · since 1989		Pulsed dyeTargets blood vessels to treat port-wine stains without scarring. source
Skin resurfacing
dermatology · since 1990s		CO2, erbium YAGScanning systems made computer-controlled resurfacing possible in the early 1990s. source
Tattoo and pigmented lesion removal
dermatology · since 2013		Picosecond (PicoSure)First picosecond laser for tattoos, FDA-cleared by January 2, 2013. source
Tattoo removal
dermatology		Q-switchedShort pulses break up tattoo ink. source

Displays		Excimer laser (line beam)Recrystallizes amorphous silicon into LTPS with up to 200x mobility for AMOLED phone displays (company claim) source
Inertial confinement fusion (NIF)
energy research · since 2022		192 beams · 2.05 MJ per ignition shot (2022)Laser-made x-rays implode a fuel capsule; first ignition December 5, 2022, 3.15 MJ out. source

Manufacturing		Fiber laser · 2-12 kW at Fabtech 2014; 30 kW called mainstream in China, 2024 (estimate)Outsold CO2 for cutting from 2015; about 200 in/min on 1/4 in mild steel at 6 kW with nitrogen (reported, 2016) source
Laser cleaning and coating removal
Manufacturing / maintenance		Pulsed laserAblates paint, rubber, insulation or rust; cleans electronics contacts and tire molds; strips paint from bridges, aircraft and ships source
