On stage: tours, festivals, companies and costs

In this cue

The short version: Pink Floyd's 1994 US tour carried two 50 W laser systems; by 2025 the Guinness record stood at 1,100 separate laser sources in one stadium. The same short list of specialist companies turns up in the credits, and they publish gear lists far more readily than prices. Ticket prices, a US paperwork fee of zero and a few manufacturer list prices are public. Day rates, insurance and the size of the whole business are not.

Which tours carried lasers, and what were the rigs?#

Gear lists come from trade magazines, which print what suppliers tell them. For how a rig like this sits inside the rest of a production, see how a dance music show is built.

  • Pink Floyd, 1994 US tour. Lighting designer Marc Brickman had "2 complete, 50W, copper-vapor laser systems", built by Oxford Lasers and supplied by Rocklite of Toronto, with Warren "Wiggy" Toll operating and Allen Domanski programming. The Lighting Dimensions article that lists them survives online as a fan-archive transcript.234
  • Roger Waters, Dark Side of the Moon tour, 2007. Partway through the tour, for the Mexico dates, Brickman added a rotating 3D prism drawn in the air by five direct-diode solid-state lasers totaling over 100 W, flown from cranes on a rig of roughly 2,500 lb. Lightwave International and Excitement Technologies Group built it; Martin Potoczny programmed and ran it on Pangolin software with grandMA playback. ILDA's Patrick Murphy told the trade press at the time: "Solid-state lasers are revolutionizing laser effects."235
  • Rush, Snakes & Arrows tour, 2007. Two Laser Scope 60 W YAG lasers and two custom projectors from Production Design International, with Andrew Seabeck as laser tech. This list also survives as a fan-archive transcript, of a PLSN profile.236
  • Tool, 2024 US and Canada tour. Lasers by Production Design International, which has worked with the band since 2009; Scott Wilson is credited as laser designer, programmer and operator.237183
  • Jean-Michel Jarre, 2017. 13 Laser Image Supernova HD 35 W OPSL lasers, 16 RGB grating boxes and a 20 W laser harp - a fan of beams wired as a MIDI controller, so that breaking a beam with a hand triggers a synthesizer note. Hugo Bunk of Laser Image supplied the lasers, run on Pangolin Beyond and FB4.238 Jarre had been working at scale for decades: his Rendez-vous Houston concert on April 5, 1986 drew 1.3 million people by PLSN's count and 1.5 million by Wikipedia's, and Guinness listed it as the largest outdoor concert.23872
  • Coldplay, Music of the Spheres. X-Laser Triton 10 (10 W) and Triton 36 (36 W) units supplied through Strictly FX. Nobody has printed how many.239
  • The 2023 arena class. PLSN's laser roundup that year listed The Weeknd's After Hours Til Dawn tour with several Kvant 25 W lasers from Pyrotecnico FX, programmed by Tyler Barbone; Drake with 10 ISFX 100 W lasers and six Kvant 30 W units from ImageSFX, four of them mounted on robots; and Aerosmith with 44 lasers of 30 W each planned. KISS's End of the Road tour, by Lightwave International's own figures, carried more than 200 lasers and 2,000 W.183

Planetarium laser shows, their music licensing and the 2026 Phoebe Bridgers "Lost Weekend" run in 44 planetariums are a separate trade with separate economics, covered in the planetarium chapter.104

What is the biggest laser show ever?#

It depends on what gets counted, and the counting unit has changed three times in eight years. The same shows sit alongside others in the landmark shows table.

Date Who Where Count Unit counted
Nov 18, 2017 ER Productions Outside the Las Vegas Convention Center, during the LDI trade show 314, at 1,377 W in the closing sequence laser fixtures249250
Oct 17, 2019 Image Engineering Staples Center, Los Angeles 642 laser beams250
2021 Dubai Racing Club Closing ceremony of the Dubai World Cup 824 as reported by Mixmag251
July 19, 2025 Verknipt ArenA Johan Cruijff ArenA, Amsterdam 1,100 laser emission sources252

The 2017 show was a seven-minute sequence, and it took about 13 tons of equipment and 6.8 miles of cable, with kit a Kent newspaper valued at £2.6 million.253254 The 2019 record was certified on site by Guinness judge Andrew Glass.250

The current record is Guinness's "Largest laser show": 1,100 laser emission sources in front of about 40,000 people. Guinness counts each point where a beam leaves a unit, and a beam split by a diffraction grating counts once.252 The record piece ran 10 minutes.251 The rig mixed RGB units above 30 W with single-color units and beam lasers, and the published safety measures read like a checklist: a risk analysis per device, a laser safety officer, compliance with DIN EN 60825-1, emergency stops, automatic beam interruption and sign-off by outside testers. The laser supplier has not been named.255

Guinness keeps a second category for ground covered. That one belongs to "Pulse of Light" by Martin Arnaud at Noor Riyadh on November 15, 2022: 14,459,160 square meters.256

The FDA's own laser light show page lists the variance forms and no fee, and the industry guide warns that a new application "can require several months" of review.28262 What the variance is and who needs one is in the rules chapter.

The only public worked example of how a laser company arrives at a day rate is an old one. L. Michael Roberts's LaserFX business pages, written between about 1996 and 2008, take a $50,000 system written off over 2.5 years: it has to earn about $20,000 a year, or roughly $1,000 per show-day at 20 shows a year. Add overhead and a 25% margin and his example lands near $2,750 per show-day, with later days at the same site billed at half.266 It shows the arithmetic in dollars of that era; it is not a quote.

No vendor publishes a current day rate for a club laser with an operator, a festival laser package, a planetarium show lease or show-control software; they quote privately. Insurance premiums for laser shows are not published anywhere citable. Nobody has produced a credible estimate of the size of the show laser business. The number that circulates - a "laser projector" market of USD 15.5 billion in 2023, forecast to reach USD 69.9 billion by 2032 - is one research firm's press-release estimate, and it describes video projectors with laser light sources for cinemas, homes and classrooms.267 It says nothing about beams in haze.

What is ILDA, and what are the ILDA Awards?#

The International Laser Display Association is the trade body, founded at an organizational meeting in Lake Tahoe on August 16, 1986, with Ron Goldstein as president, Walt Meador as secretary and Tim Walsh as treasurer.74 It has four membership categories - corporate, nonprofit, individual and student - and members get awards eligibility, conference access, free use of the Skyzan laser safety software, the member logo and recourse to an ethics committee.268

The ILDA Awards are the industry's annual prizes; how they are judged is in the programming chapter. The artistic categories include Beams/Atmospherics, Graphics, Abstract, Planetarium, Corporate, Live Stage, Live TV, Laser Photography and Laser Jockey. The Fenning Award goes to technical achievement, and the Career Achievement Award is the highest honor. In 2014 there were 204 entries from 38 member companies, a record number of entrants, and the ceremony was held in Las Vegas on November 19.261 The first Career Achievement Award went to Laserium's founder Ivan Dryer; ILDA's own page gives the year as both 1988 and 1989.269

ILDA also runs an accreditation, the ILDA Accredited Professional Lasershow Company. A company needs at least 15 documented shows over two years, an award or two award entries, three client references, an employee who has passed a Laser Safety Officer course and a signed safety affirmation. ILDA membership is not required. Five companies hold it: Lightwave International, LOBO electronic, Laser Production Network, Laser Spectacles and Dynamic Lasers.265 It is an industry credential, separate from any government permission.

Is laser art in galleries a different thing from a laser show?#

Same light, different job.

Rockne Krebs (1938-2011) moved from Plexiglas sculpture to laser light in 1967 with "Sculpture Minus Object" at the Washington Gallery of Modern Art: red beams, fog and mirrors, and nothing else in the room. He worked with Hewlett-Packard physicists in 1969, showed in the US pavilion at Expo '70 in Osaka, and went on to make more than 30 large public light works.270 In September 1969 he filed US patent 3,622,228, which ILDA calls apparently the first patent for laser artistic display. His 1983 piece "The Green Hypotenuse" ran a single beam 7 miles from Mount Wilson to Caltech.38

Hiro Yamagata put "Photon 999" on the Guggenheim Museum Bilbao from October 31 to December 2, 2001: 15 lasers projecting onto a structure on the facade, with a walkway through the light.271 The beams, from tarm Showlaser, Times of Change and Dymax, were aimed at more than 200 mirrors on 20 m truss towers.272

Yvette Mattern's "Global Rainbow" is seven parallel beams in the colors of the spectrum, first shown in New York in 2009 and since taken to Berlin, three UK sites for the London 2012 Cultural Olympiad, Toronto's Nuit Blanche in 2014 and Pittsburgh, where Lightwave International supplied the lasers.273258

Chris Levine's "Higher Power" sent one green 515 nm beam from San Clemente island across Venice from May 4 to 11, 2026, during the Biennale's opening week.274 Rita McBride's "Portal", at the 2016 Liverpool Biennial, crossed beams inside the Toxteth Reservoir; the projector maker Kvant says the piece used 16 green modules of 1 W each at 520 nm.275

Quick answers

What is the biggest laser show ever?
The current Guinness record for largest laser show is 1,100 laser emission sources, set by Verknipt ArenA at the Johan Cruijff ArenA in Amsterdam on July 19, 2025. Earlier records counted different things: 314 fixtures in Las Vegas in 2017 and 642 beams in Los Angeles in 2019. A separate Guinness record for area covered belongs to a 2022 show at Noor Riyadh.
How much does a laser show cost to hire?
Laser companies quote privately, and no current day rates are published. The only public worked example, from about 2008, takes a $50,000 system and arrives at roughly $1,000 to $2,750 per show-day. Hardware has some visible prices: in 2026 a 10 W projector carried a manufacturer metadata price of 8,582 EUR before VAT.
Is the Sphere a laser show?
Sphere's own display is an LED screen, but touring acts do bring lasers in. Illenium's 2026 "Odyssey" residency used 60 W projectors across the stage front and 20 projectors of 58 W placed around the room.
