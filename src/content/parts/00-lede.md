# The Beam

On November 19, 1973, a filmmaker named Ivan Dryer switched on a krypton laser under the dome of Griffith Observatory in Los Angeles, and a person at a console played it live to recorded music while the stars turned overhead.[^his-griffith-timeline][^his-imersa-lightyears] People have been walking out of planetariums asking how that works ever since.

The answer is smaller than it looks. A laser show is one dot of colored light, steered by two mirrors the size of a fingernail, moving fast enough that your eye draws the lines for it. Everything else is detail: where the light comes from, who decides where the dot goes, and what keeps it out of your eye.

That last part is why this is a regulated trade and not a hobby aisle. In the United States a show laser above 5 mW of visible output needs a variance from the FDA.[^saf-fda-shows] A festival rig can run a thousand of them: the Guinness record, set in 2025, is 1,100 laser sources in one stadium.[^biz-guinness-largest]
