Lasers at festivals and big events

The short version: A festival laser rig is mostly a list of Kvant model numbers and a software license, and most of what is known about any rig comes from the company that sold the projectors or the software. The best documented nights are Coachella 2025, Glastonbury 2019, Illenium's 147-laser stadium show in 2024 and a run of Super Bowl halftimes. Outdoors in the US, every one of them also needed a letter from the FAA, and whether the beams may touch the crowd depends on which side of the Atlantic the field is on.

The headline counts for EDC, the halftimes and the Guinness records, and the companies behind them, are in /stage/. What follows is the detail.

Who does the lasers for Coachella?#

In 2025, Polar Productions, on six stages and at Stagecoach. It lit Quasar, Sahara, Yuma, the main stage, Gobi and Mojave, and put lasers into Stagecoach for the first time, at Diplo's Honky Tonk.244

The model list reads like a Kvant catalog: Atom 42, BeamBrush 35, BeamBrush 10 with PASS, and Clubmax 24, 10 and 6800 with PASS, all run from Pangolin's BEYOND software and FB4 network hardware.244 PASS, the Professional Audience Safety System, is what let selected units on the main stage, Gobi and Mojave point into the crowd. Quasar used six Kvant Atom units in a circle as an aerial marker.244 The case study is Pangolin's own account, and it gives no wattages, no total count and no designers.

Three years earlier the headline laser rig came with the act. Swedish House Mafia's Coachella 2022 lasers were supplied by ER Productions and programmed by Derek Abbott in BEYOND, with the Depence visualizer and FB4. The projectors sat in a circular structure over the DJs to throw aerial beams, and the design went on tour afterward. That account is also Pangolin's.276

How many lasers were at EDC and Ultra?#

At EDC Las Vegas in 2014, the Kinetic Field main stage ran more than 30 high-power laser systems. Lasertainment, Laserworld's show partner, supplied RTI NANO and RTI PIKO projectors, controlled by a Lasergraph DSP with a grandMA2 lighting console on top. No wattage, no designer.241

Miami's Ultra Music Festival published a similar number a year later. In 2015 LaserNet (Laser Production Network), a Miami company, ran "over 30 laser projectors" across seven stages at Bayfront Park, every one above 20 W and every one on BEYOND.277 The source is Pangolin's own case study, and it names no models.

What lasers did Illenium use at SoFi Stadium?#

On February 2 and 3, 2024, Illenium played his Trilogy shows at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles.279 Pangolin's case study puts the rig at 147 laser systems totaling more than 4,000 W. Most were Kvant Atom 42s, with BeamBrush 35, Clubmax 6800 and 6000 units and a featured Kvant EPIC rated at 270 W. Brad Kellogg was lead designer and Bill Loftus laser programmer, working with Polar Productions.280

The same page calls it "the highest wattage laser show ever witnessed".280 That is the vendor's line, not a certified record. Nobody certifies watts; the Guinness record a year later counted emission sources instead.

Swedish House Mafia's "3.0 era" opened in Mexico City in 2025 at the Plaza de Toros with 12 Kvant Atom 58, 12 Atom 42 and two Spectrum 55 units, 26 in all. InterlaserMX designed, programmed and operated it for the promoter OCESA, by Pangolin's account.281 A smaller bass festival shows the same supply chain at another scale: Pangolin says Forbidden Kingdom 2026 ran Kvant Spectrum 25 and 30 and Clubmax 40 units through Hypnotic Productions, a South Florida firm, with no counts.282

Do lasers at Sphere hit the screen?#

Some are aimed at it, and some are aimed to stop. For Illenium's "Odyssey" at Sphere in Las Vegas, a show the trade write-up does not date, 16 Kvant Atom 58 W projectors were "positioned to interact with the venue's immersive screen", and four more on the lower mezzanine handled beam termination - ending beams on a surface so they go no farther. The lasers were zoned so the beams looked symmetrical from every seat. Bill Loftus did system design, programming and operation.245

Anyma's "The End of Genesys" ran at Sphere from February 27 to March 2, 2025, with "sharp, precise laser beams" moving with the drops, in Digital Music News's description. No supplier is named.283

What does a Super Bowl halftime laser rig look like?#

Wet, mostly. For The Who at Super Bowl XLIV, Sun Life Stadium in Miami, February 7, 2010, Laser Design Productions ran 16 of its LDP 10 projectors: four at 50 W, four at 25 W, four at 20 W and four at 13 W, which adds to about 432 W. The trade report rounds it to more than 400 W. Doug Adams, the company's president, designed it, Victor Tomei was laser technical director and Jason McEachern programmed. Rain housings, vibration-isolating platforms and generators kept the gear alive on a football field.284 Pangolin's account of the same night, in the stage table, splits the 16 another way: eight full-color units on radial carts, four 50 W YAGs and four air-cooled full-color units.240

For Usher at Super Bowl LVIII in 2024, Pangolin's case study adds the lighting credits to its BeamBrush 40 rig: Al Gurdon as show designer, Eric Marchwinski and Mark Humphrey as lighting directors and programmers, Harry Forster and Ben Green as lighting designers.243

Regular-season football has caught up. On December 28, 2025, the San Francisco 49ers ran an eight-minute "Bay Area Rock & Roll Laser Halftime Show" at Levi's Stadium during a game against the Chicago Bears, designed and produced by Image Engineering. The stadium's all-LED field lighting could switch between game and show almost instantly, and the team's Laura Johnson noted that the show "doesn't require anyone to be on the field".285

Does Disney still use lasers in its nighttime shows?#

At Disneyland Paris, yes, and with secondhand lasers of some pedigree. At the Paris 2024 opening ceremony on July 26, the Eiffel Tower carried "lasers and a custom spotlight package".247 In January 2025, AP reported that "Disney Tales of Magic" reuses lasers from the tower that night. The 20-minute show adds drones, fountains and projection, Tim Lutkin is artistic director, and AP says the beams "travel 23 miles (37 km)".286

What were the biggest New Year's Eve laser shows?#

Dubai's, by the official measure. For "Light Up 2018", Emaar replaced the Burj Khalifa fireworks with a light and laser show of about seven minutes. Guinness certified it as the "largest light and sound show on a single building", covering 109,252 square meters, more than double the previous record set on Hong Kong's ICC tower in 2013.287 Five years later Emaar announced that its 2022-23 show would set "a new world record for the largest laser display". That was a pre-event announcement, with no laser count or supplier.288

The industry's own prize went somewhere colder. ILDA's 2026 Corporate Show award went to "New Years Eve Eskilstuna 2026", by Merlin Schaadt and Laserimage AB of Sweden.289 Where lasers have replaced fireworks outright, and why, is in /now/.

What is Vivid Sydney's laser show?#

"Laser Lightfall", over the water at Cockle Bay, billed as the first Vivid Sydney Laser Show and "Australia's most ambitious free outdoor laser spectacular". Destination NSW produced it, ER Productions from the UK made the laser show, and Sonar Music (Antony Partos and Jono Ma) wrote the score. It ran 23 nights from May 28 to June 19, 2026: seven-minute shows, four an hour from 6 p.m. to 11 p.m., with some nights given over to a "Sky Symphony" version with fireworks.290

How does a festival laser department work?#

The laser company sends its own Laser Safety Officer (LSO), usually the person at the controls. Roberta McHatton, an LSO writing for the Event Safety Alliance in 2024, puts the job in one line: "The operator is responsible for stopping laser emission whenever a hazard is present!"177

Her riskiest hour is setup, not the show, when crew are in places nobody can predict and the audience has not arrived. Her checklist covers projector mounts and clamps (including on lifts that vibrate), fixed bounce mirrors, emission indicators, interlocks, beam attenuators and labels, and anything reflective in the venue. Crews align at the lowest power, lock the zones and post warning signs while they work. Zoning, software limits that keep beams out of set areas, counts as an engineering control, and the operator still watches for balloons and crowd surfers. Her rule of thumb puts lasers at least 3 m above audience feet.177

In the UK, HSE's leaflet INDG224 puts the duty to assess and control the risk on the organizer, who is expected to check that a hired laser company is competent. It lists the engineering controls - enclosures, blanking plates, scan-failure systems, emergency stops - and describes display lasers as running from about 200 mW to 40 W and up, able to burn skin and start fires above roughly 500 mW.30 Who carries which legal role, country by country, is in /rules/. The rest of a festival's machinery is in how festivals work.

Do outdoor laser shows need FAA permission?#

In the US the FAA does not permit shows; it reviews them. Under Advisory Circular 70-1B (2022), anyone sending visible beams above 50 nW/cm2 into navigable airspace, or any beam above the exposure limit, files Form 7140-1 at least 30 days ahead, with a Laser Configuration Worksheet for each setup and beam-path diagrams. The answer is a letter of determination: no objection, or objection.215 ILDA's one-page overview tells its members to file 90 days out, says review takes 30 to 90 days, and says the paperwork and the letter should be on site on show day.178

Near airports the brightness caps fall by zone, down to 50 nW/cm2 in the Laser-Free Zone. The circular's control measures include safety observers who can cut the beam, beam termination on surfaces, and radar or imaging; an automated system standing in for observers has to meet SAE AS6029.215 In the UK, HSE points organizers of outdoor shows to Civil Aviation Authority guidance and a NOTAM.30

Why do the beams go over the crowd in the US and into it in Europe?#

Because the default flips. ILDA's US overview says beams normally stay 3 m above the standing surface and 2.5 m to the side of it, unless the FDA has approved audience scanning in the show's variance.178 With that approval, it happens: the PASS units at Coachella 2025 were there for it.244

Europe is not one rule. In the UK the organizer carries the duty under general health and safety law, with PLASA's free, HSE-endorsed guidance standing in for the withdrawn HS(G)95, and the leaflet sets no numeric audience limit.30 The Nordic radiation safety authorities went the other way on June 20, 2017, jointly recommending that audiences not be exposed to high-power lasers at all. They counted diffraction gratings as nearly as risky, and where exposure happens anyway they want it below the eye limit, normally 2.55 mW/cm2 for a static beam or 1 mW through a 7 mm aperture, with continuous-wave lasers only, a measurement before every show at the worst spot, and everything written down.291 How a beam is kept under the limit is in /safety/.

What isn't public?#

The rig lists for several of the biggest stages in the world. Nobody has published a projector list or supplier for Kinetic Field since 2019, for Ultra's main stage since 2015, or for Tomorrowland's main stage in any year. The Paris 2024 tower lasers have a second career at Disney and still no published supplier or count. Most of what exists was published by vendors with something to sell, so Kvant and Pangolin turn up partly because those companies write their jobs down.

Quick answers

Who does the lasers for Coachella?
In 2025 Polar Productions supplied lasers to six Coachella stages and to Stagecoach, using Kvant projectors run on Pangolin BEYOND and FB4. Selected units on the main stage, Gobi and Mojave carried PASS hardware for audience scanning.
How many lasers does EDC Kinetic Field use?
More than 30 high-power laser systems ran on Kinetic Field in 2014, supplied by Lasertainment. Nobody has published a Kinetic Field projector list for recent years.
What lasers did Illenium use at SoFi Stadium?
For the Trilogy shows on February 2 and 3, 2024, the software maker's case study lists 147 laser systems totaling more than 4,000 W, mostly Kvant Atom 42s plus a 270 W Kvant EPIC. Brad Kellogg designed it and Bill Loftus programmed it, with Polar Productions.
Do lasers at Sphere Las Vegas hit the screen?
For Illenium's Odyssey at Sphere, 16 Kvant Atom 58 W units were placed to interact with the screen and four projectors on the lower mezzanine served as beam terminations. The lasers were zoned so beams looked symmetrical from every angle.
Do outdoor laser shows need FAA permission?
The FAA does not permit shows, but under Advisory Circular 70-1B an operator files Form 7140-1 at least 30 days ahead and gets a letter saying the FAA objects or does not. ILDA advises its members to file 90 days ahead.
Why do lasers go over the crowd in the US but into it in Europe?
In the US, beams normally stay 3 m above and 2.5 m beside the audience unless the FDA has approved audience scanning in the show's variance. Europe varies: the UK leaves the duty with the organizer under health and safety law, while the Nordic regulators recommend against audience exposure altogether.
