1998 · Morris County, New Jersey · New Jersey Superior Court, Law Division		Reliability of a police laser speed gun as evidenceReadings admissible without expert testimony under stated conditions (day or night, light or moderately heavy rain, up to 1,000 feet); heavy rain, falling snow or longer range need expert testimony source
